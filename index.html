The Problem

Your team adopted the tools.

How We Work

Here is exactly what working
with us looks like.

Five phases. A tangible output at every stage. Nothing begins until the previous phase is complete and agreed.

01

Discovery

1–2 weeks

We spend structured time inside your organization — asking the right questions across departments, mapping what actually happens versus what is assumed. Nothing is templated. Every engagement starts from zero.

You receive A complete map of where your organization is losing time and why — with specific friction points ranked by impact.
02

Workflow Analysis

1–2 weeks

We identify which workflows are AI-ready, which need redesigning first, and which should stay untouched. Prioritized by business impact — not by what is technically interesting.

You receive A prioritized AI opportunity matrix — what to automate, what to redesign, what to leave as-is, and in what sequence.
03

AI Strategy

1 week

A concrete plan — tools, integration architecture, success metrics, and timelines — agreed in writing before any build begins. No vague proposals. No surprises mid-engagement.

You receive An integration blueprint with agreed success metrics. You know exactly what you are getting before a line of code is written.
04

Implementation

4–8 weeks

We build and integrate. Production-quality systems connected to your existing stack — ERP, LMS, communication tools, data layers — documented and tested before handover. Your team is trained before we leave.

You receive A live AI-integrated workflow system. Full documentation. Trained staff. You own it completely — no ongoing dependency on us.
05

Optimization

Ongoing

After deployment, we measure against the original success metrics, fix what needs fixing, and ensure the system improves — not degrades — over the first 90 days.

You receive A performance report measured against the original goals. Iteration plan for the next quarter.

Common Questions

Questions we get asked
before every first call.

Is KapoorAI a software company or a consultancy?

Both. We consult — mapping workflows and designing AI strategy — and we build — integrating AI into production systems and shipping products. The distinction matters because we don't just hand you a recommendation document. We stay until the system is live and your team is trained on it.

How long does a typical engagement take?

Discovery runs 1–2 weeks. A full implementation engagement typically takes 8–14 weeks from first call to handover, depending on the scope and number of workflows involved. We will give you a specific estimate after the discovery call — not before, because we don't know your workflows yet.

How is KapoorAI different from an AI agency?

Most AI agencies start by choosing a tool and then figure out where to apply it. We start by mapping your workflows, identifying where the real friction is, and only then selecting the right technology. The difference shows up in adoption — AI built onto a redesigned workflow gets used. AI bolted onto an old one doesn't.

What happens after the engagement ends?

You own everything. Every system we build is fully documented, and your team is trained before we hand over. We do not create systems that require our ongoing presence to function. If you want post-engagement support, we offer it — but you will not need it as a condition of the system working.

Do you work with organizations our size?

We have worked with individual schools, college groups, and SMEs. Enterprise engagements are beginning. The methodology scales — what changes is the scope of each phase, not the sequence. If you are uncertain whether we are the right fit, the discovery call will tell you that within 30 minutes.

What does the discovery call cost?

Nothing. The 30-minute discovery call is free and obligation-free. If we are not the right fit for your situation, we will tell you honestly — and point you toward someone who might be. We would rather have that conversation than begin an engagement that is not right for either side.
